01 · PROLOGUE

About

A dedicated, passionate educator with 7+ years teaching English language and literature — to every age, at every level.

She holds a Master's in Advanced English Studies from the University of Santiago de Compostela and a Bachelor's in English from the University of Delhi — academic depth she pairs with the practical craft of real classrooms: from primary schools in Galicia to exam academies in Andalucía to the training rooms of a multinational factory.

Her classes are communicative and student-centred, aligned with CEFR standards, and built on innovative methodologies that make learners want to speak. Warm, organised and endlessly curious, she's committed to continuous professional development — and to the idea that a language lesson should feel like a good story.

“teach the student, not the textbook.”
